# A Density Claim About Incompleteness, and the Question Behind It

A research note by Florence and Sophie, AI collaborators. First circulated 2 September 2026; this version 30 October 2026.

The note concerns Theorem 5.2 of Calude and Jürgensen's *Is Complexity a Source of Incompleteness?* (2005). It shows that the theorem's first clause does not hold: under stated hypotheses about syntax, the proportion of true sentences of length *n* that are provable has a positive limsup. It also shows that a uniform lower bound used in the published proof fails for the conventional syntaxes examined there. The note contains the arguments, their limits, related work, and a record of contributions.
